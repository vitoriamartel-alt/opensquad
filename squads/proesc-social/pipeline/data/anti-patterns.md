# Anti-Patterns — Proesc Social Media Content

> Common mistakes to avoid. These are based on B2B EdTech industry research and competitor analysis.

---

## Content Anti-Patterns

### 1. Generic Educational Content
**The mistake:** Posting about "the importance of technology in education" or "how digital transformation helps schools" — content that could be published by ANY company.
**Why it's harmful:** Zero differentiation. School managers have seen this content 1,000 times. They ignore it.
**Instead:** Always anchor to a specific, concrete problem Proesc solves. "Como automatizar 80% das cobranças de mensalidade" > "A tecnologia transforma a gestão escolar."

### 2. Feature-First Positioning
**The mistake:** Leading with "A Proesc tem uma funcionalidade incrível que..."
**Why it's harmful:** No one cares about features. They care about outcomes. Features without context are noise.
**Instead:** Lead with the problem. "Gestores perdem em média 14h/mês em cobranças manuais — a Proesc resolve isso em 30 minutos."

### 3. Vague Social Proof
**The mistake:** "Nossos clientes adoram a Proesc!" or "1.500 escolas confiam em nós."
**Why it's harmful:** Numbers without context are meaningless. 1.500 schools could mean 1.500 tiny preschools.
**Instead:** Specific, verifiable social proof. "A Escola X em [cidade] reduziu inadimplência de 23% para 8% em 4 meses com o sistema de cobrança automatizada da Proesc."

### 4. Over-Posting Promotional Content
**The mistake:** Every post is "Conheça a Proesc" or "Solicite uma demo."
**Why it's harmful:** B2B audiences on social media are not in buy mode. They're in learn mode. Over-promotion triggers unfollows.
**Instead:** 80/20 rule — 80% educational/value content, 20% promotional.

### 5. Ignoring Platform Context
**The mistake:** Posting the same caption on Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Facebook.
**Why it's harmful:** Each platform has a different algorithm, user behavior, and optimal format. Cross-posting without adaptation tanks performance on every platform.
**Instead:** Adapt the angle, format, and length for each platform. Same pauta, different execution.

### 6. Missing the Seasonal Context
**The mistake:** Posting about rematrícula content in November, or enrollment content in June.
**Why it's harmful:** School managers' pain points change radically by season. Off-season content feels irrelevant.
**Instead:** Always check the school calendar context. What is the manager dealing with RIGHT NOW?

## Copy Anti-Patterns

### 7. Weak Hooks
**The mistake:** "Olá, gestores! Hoje vamos falar sobre..." or "Você sabia que a gestão escolar...?"
**Why it's harmful:** These openings signal low-effort content. The manager scrolls past before reading.
**Instead:** Start with the tension. "Sua escola perde R$3.000/mês sem saber." Jump into the problem on line 1.

### 8. Hashtag Spam
**The mistake:** Using all 30 Instagram hashtags with irrelevant tags like #escola #educação #professor.
**Why it's harmful:** Algorithm suppresses posts with irrelevant hashtags. Looks unprofessional.
**Instead:** 5-15 relevant hashtags. Mix of niche (#gestaoescolar, #sistemaescolar), mid-range (#diretordeescola), and brand (#proesc).

### 9. CTA-less Content
**The mistake:** Ending posts with "Esperamos ter ajudado! Até a próxima."
**Why it's harmful:** Zero direction for the audience. Content is forgotten immediately.
**Instead:** Every post ends with ONE specific action: "Comenta aqui o maior desafio da sua escola", "Salva esse carrossel para consultar depois", "Link nos comentários para agendar uma demo gratuita."

---

## Always Do

1. **Lead with the pain point.** School managers are busy and skeptical. Show you understand their problem before offering the solution.

2. **Use real numbers.** "Economize tempo" < "Economize 6 horas por semana". Always quantify.

3. **Name the audience.** "Gestores escolares", "Diretores de escola", "Administradores escolares" — being specific makes managers feel seen.

4. **Adapt for the platform.** What works on LinkedIn (long-form, first-person insights) does not work on TikTok (3-second hook, visual storytelling).

5. **Review for accuracy before publishing.** One wrong statistic can damage Proesc's credibility with an audience that values data and precision.
