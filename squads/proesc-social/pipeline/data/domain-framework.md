# Domain Framework — Proesc Social Media Content Production

> Operational framework for producing B2B EdTech social media content for school managers.

---

## The Proesc Content Cycle

```
Pauta → Ângulo → Copy → Revisão → Publicação
```

### Step 1: Research & Source Selection
- Monitor trending topics in Brazilian education (MEC, INEP, CNE, education media)
- Track competitor content for gaps and opportunities
- Identify what school managers are discussing (LinkedIn groups, forums, WhatsApp groups)
- Prioritize data-backed stories and case studies over opinion pieces
- **Output:** Ranked list of 3-5 pautas with source links

### Step 2: Angle Generation
- For each pauta, generate 5 distinct emotional lenses:
  1. 🔴 **Medo** — "what you risk by NOT doing this"
  2. 🟢 **Oportunidade** — "this is your window before everyone catches on"
  3. 📚 **Educacional** — "here's how it actually works"
  4. ↔️ **Contrário** — "the popular opinion is wrong"
  5. ⭐ **Inspiracional** — "imagine your school after this change"
- Each angle produces a completely different piece of content from the same source
- **Output:** 5 ângulos with hook and angle summary

### Step 3: Platform Content Creation
- Instagram Feed: 8-10 slide carousel (Problema→Solução or Listicle preferred)
- LinkedIn: Text post with 5 insights (document carousel for higher reach)
- YouTube: Script structure Hook → Problem → Solution → Demo → CTA
- Facebook: Community-focused version of Instagram content
- TikTok: 60s vertical video script with 3-second hook
- **Output:** Complete copy for each platform

### Step 4: Quality Review
- Check brand voice alignment (Proesc tone guidelines)
- Verify all claims are accurate and sourced
- Validate platform-specific formatting rules
- Score against quality criteria
- **Output:** Scored review with APPROVE or REVISION_NEEDED + feedback

### Step 5: Packaging
- Organize all platform outputs in structured format
- Generate publishing checklist with optimal times
- Create hashtag sets per platform
- **Output:** Complete content package ready for scheduling

## Seasonal Triggers for Proesc Content

- **Jan-Fev:** Matrícula season — focus on enrollment automation
- **Mar-Abr:** Back to school — pedagogical management content
- **Mai-Jun:** Mid-year — financial management, billing, retention
- **Jul:** Rematrícula season — re-enrollment features
- **Ago-Set:** Planning season — calendar and academic planning
- **Out-Nov:** Vestibular / End of year events
- **Dez:** Year-end reports, success stories, look-ahead
